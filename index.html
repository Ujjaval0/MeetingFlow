The Founder's Story

A letter from the creator on how MeetingFlow was born out of frustration.

SC

Sarah Connor

Founder & Chief Architect

GitHub LinkedIn
"We spent more time documenting what we had already discussed than actually building the product. I knew we had to automate the overhead so developers could focus on coding."

As a product architect at fast-scaling startups, I noticed a repeating pattern: teams would have a brilliant 30-minute sync, only to spend the next two hours arguing in Slack threads about what was actually decided, who owned which tasks, and when they were due.

Frustrated by tools that simply threw raw, unformatted transcripts at teams, I sat down in late 2024 to write a simple parser. I wanted a tool that didn't just listen, but actually understood the engineering requirements, extracted actionable checkboxes, and synced them automatically to our issue boards.

Today, MeetingFlow is built by a small team of three engineering and design minds. We are committed to creating interfaces that respect your attention, maintain absolute data privacy, and translate voice discussions into structured execution loops instantly.

Cheers,

Sarah Connor
